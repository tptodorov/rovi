What you are building

  1. A perfboard carrier holds the ESP32 and both TB6612FNG drivers in plug-in sockets.
  2. The four motors and the motor power bank connect with latching JST-SM plugs.
  3. The ESP32 has its own power bank on its USB-C port; the grounds join on the carrier.
  4. Every wire on the carrier is in the generated wire list, which is proven to match the KiCad schematic.
Tip: Work top to bottom. Tick each box as you go; ticks are saved in this browser.

1. Gather the parts

Note: Tools: soldering iron, solder, SN-28B type crimper, wire stripper, flush cutters, multimeter, heat gun or lighter.

2. Make the four motor harnesses

Why: The side that carries power gets the recessed sockets, so an unplugged live plug can't short. A reversed motor only spins backwards; the firmware inverts it.

3. Crimp each terminal

Crimping with an SN-28B ratchet crimper
Tip: A bad crimp costs a terminal; a hidden bad crimp costs an evening. Redo any doubtful one.

4. Make the motor power cable

Why: 3 pins can't mate with a 2-pin motor plug, and the keyed housing only fits one way round.

5. Dry-fit the carrier (no solder)

Careful: If your boards' header spacing is not 10 and 6 holes, stop: the layout is regenerated from one table.
