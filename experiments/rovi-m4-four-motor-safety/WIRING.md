# M4 wiring: four motors, two TB6612FNG drivers, two ribbon cables

Schematic: [`wiring.kicad_sch`](wiring.kicad_sch), generated by [`tools/gen_wiring.py`](tools/gen_wiring.py) (edit the generator, not the schematic; `--check` compares KiCad's netlist with the intended nets: 26 nets, ERC 0 errors). Every connection is a drawn wire. To build it on a perfboard with latching plugs, follow the [build guide](build/README.md). No current monitor, dead-man or bench supply ([ADR-0014](../../docs/adr/0014-m4-minimal-wiring-software-fail-safe.md)); just decoupling capacitors.

Each driver's control header (right header R1-R7: `PWMA, AIN2, AIN1, STBY, BIN1, BIN2, PWMB`) takes **7 consecutive ESP32 header pins**, so each driver is one **7-wire ribbon cable**, laid straight: ESP32 pin 1 of the run to driver `R1`, and so on. This replaces M1's pin roles (M1 had `STBY` on GPIO4); the firmware follows this table.

## Ribbon 1: ESP32 left header → driver 1 (left wheels)

| Wire | ESP32 header | GPIO | Driver 1 pin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | L4 | 4 | R1 `PWMA` |
| 2 | L5 | 5 | R2 `AIN2` |
| 3 | L6 | 6 | R3 `AIN1` |
| 4 | L7 | 7 | R4 `STBY` |
| 5 | L8 | 15 | R5 `BIN1` |
| 6 | L9 | 16 | R6 `BIN2` |
| 7 | L10 | 17 | R7 `PWMB` |

## Ribbon 2: ESP32 left header L14-L20 → driver 2 (right wheels)

| Wire | ESP32 header | GPIO | Driver 2 pin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | L14 | 46 | R1 `PWMA` |
| 2 | L15 | 9 | R2 `AIN2` |
| 3 | L16 | 10 | R3 `AIN1` |
| 4 | L17 | 11 | R4 `STBY` |
| 5 | L18 | 12 | R5 `BIN1` |
| 6 | L19 | 13 | R6 `BIN2` |
| 7 | L20 | 14 | R7 `PWMB` |

L11 (GPIO18), L12 (GPIO8) and L13 (GPIO3) stay free between the two ribbons.

## Power, ground and motors (single wires)

Two 5 V USB power banks: **BT1 for the motors**, **BT2 for the ESP32** (through the board's USB-C COM port). Separate banks keep motor starts and stalls off the ESP32's supply; the grounds are joined through the GND wires below.

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| ESP32 power bank (BT2) | ESP32 USB-C COM port (USB cable) |
| ESP32 `3V3` (L1) | Driver 1 `VCC` (L2) |
| ESP32 `3V3` (L2) | Driver 2 `VCC` (L2) |
| ESP32 `GND` (R1, right header) | Driver 1 `GND` (L3) |
| ESP32 `GND` (L22) | Driver 2 `GND` (L3) |
| Motor power bank (BT1) + 5 V | Driver 1 `VM` (L1) and driver 2 `VM` (L1) |
| Motor power bank (BT1) − | Driver 1 `GND` and driver 2 `GND` |
| Driver 1 `AO1`/`AO2` (L4/L5) | front-left motor |
| Driver 1 `BO1`/`BO2` (L7/L6) | rear-left motor |
| Driver 2 `AO1`/`AO2` (L4/L5) | front-right motor |
| Driver 2 `BO1`/`BO2` (L7/L6) | rear-right motor |

The drivers' other `GND` pins (L8, R8) are tied to L3 on the board and stay free.

## Capacitors

| Ref | Part | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | 220-470 µF electrolytic, ≥ 10 V | Driver 1 `VM` to `GND`, as close to the driver as possible. **Polarity: stripe (−) to GND** |
| C2 | 220-470 µF electrolytic, ≥ 10 V | Driver 2 `VM` to `GND`, same |
| C3-C6 | 100 nF ceramic (marked `104`), any polarity | Soldered across each motor's two terminals (M1-M4) |

C1/C2 absorb the motors' current steps so the driver's supply stays steady; C3-C6 suppress brush noise at the source, which helps the radio. If only electrolytics are at hand, fit C1/C2 first.

## Notes

* **Why these pins:** the two runs of 7 consecutive usable pins on the left header. L4-L10 (GPIO4-7, 15-17) and L14-L20 (GPIO46, 9-14) only show a 60 µs low glitch at power-up, which cannot enable a driver. L11 (GPIO18, high glitch) and L13 (GPIO3, strapping) are skipped. GPIO46 is a strapping pin too, but its reset state is an internal pull-down, which is also the boot default, and the TB6612 input's own pull-down agrees; after boot it is driver 2's `PWMA`, inert while `STBY` is low ([SoC datasheet](../../docs/reference/esp32-s3-n16r8/soc-datasheet.md) pin table and Table 2-2).
* **STBY:** one per driver (GPIO7, GPIO11), both switched together: low from boot and unless armed. In reset or with the ESP32 unpowered, the TB6612's internal pull-down holds it low. The 1 s RTC watchdog resets the chip if the control tick stops.
* **PWM:** MCPWM0 at 20 kHz: operator 0 drives driver 1 `PWMA`/`PWMB` (GPIO4/17), operator 1 drives driver 2 `PWMA`/`PWMB` (GPIO46/14).
* **Firmware mapping** (`CHANNELS` in `../rovi-m8-setpoint-ros/firmware/src/fw.rs`): driver 1 A = front-left, 1 B = rear-left, 2 A = front-right, 2 B = rear-right; no motor inverted yet. The right motors face the other way, so they will likely turn backwards at first; step 2's first test records that, and the fix is one `invert: true` per motor. Motor wire polarity doesn't matter for safety.
* **Kill switch:** unplugging the motor power bank (BT1) stops every motor; the ESP32 stays up on BT2. Four motors draw about 0.6 A running. Some power banks switch off at very low current: the motor bank may turn itself off while the motors are idle (step 2 checks it).
