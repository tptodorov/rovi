# 2026-10-14 start and keep-running duty, one motor

Setup as step 1 (M1's wiring, power bank 5 V on VM, motor free on the table, no load). M8 firmware, duty = `vx` × 400 ‰ (cap 800 ‰ at wheel command 1.0). Steps called out on the laptop speaker; Todor observed. Log: `car.log`.

* **Start from standstill:** 2-20 % in steps: first reaction at 8 %. Fine pass 6, 7, 8, 9 %: **8 % twitches, 9 % turns.**
* **Keep running:** one stream from 12 % down to 2 %, 1 % per second: smooth, **stopped at about 7 %**.
* So with no load, the motor starts at 9 % and keeps turning down to about 8 %. Under load on the floor both will be higher (M5).
