This page reads phone sensors and previews their meaning. It cannot connect to Rovi or send motor commands. The car can stay switched off.

Use Bluefy. Hold the phone in portrait, screen facing up like a tray, with the top edge pointing away. Keep that posture throughout a trial.

α is heading, β forward/back tilt, γ side tilt. Heading is relative to calibration, not a compass. Missing axes or readings older than 250 ms block the preview.

Local preview only: 3° dead zone, full scale at 20°. These trial values and Euler-angle mapping are unvalidated. Multiple axes may combine here; nothing is transmitted.

Test sequence

  1. Enable and calibrate neutral. Stay still for 60 seconds, then save the stationary result to measure jitter and drift.
  2. Hold preview. Tip forward/back, then left/right. Return to neutral after each move.
  3. Turn the whole phone left/right like a tray. Hold each heading for 10 seconds. The rotation preview should stay steady, then return to zero at neutral.
  4. Combine tilt and turning. Check that each preview axis matches your intention and that releasing the pad immediately clears all three.
  5. Tap a stall button, then hold preview before its two-second countdown ends. Preview must clear when freshness expires. Resume and recalibrate before testing the other stall.
  6. Test app switch, lock/unlock and screen rotation. Preview must clear and require enabling/calibrating again.

Stall simulation continues the 50 ms local consumer. Resuming input never restores an old calibration or hold. These checks do not measure rover stop latency.
